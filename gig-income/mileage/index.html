Mileage FAQ

Does tracking start automatically?

No. You start and stop mileage tracking manually, so you decide which trips are recorded as work.

Can I pause a trip?

Yes. You can pause and resume a trip when your workday includes a break.

Can I add mileage after the fact?

Yes. Manual trip entry lets you add date, times, duration and miles without a GPS route.

Can one trip connect to more than one gig platform?

Yes. A trip can be linked to income from multiple platforms without duplicating the trip's miles.

Can one income entry include multiple trips?

Yes. This is useful when one income entry covers separate work periods or split shifts.

Why might an address or route be missing?

Manual trips may not contain GPS data, and address availability depends on saved location information and geocoding.

Keep mileage with the rest of your gig records

GIG Income brings work miles, time, income, expenses and multi-platform statistics together instead of splitting them across separate apps.
